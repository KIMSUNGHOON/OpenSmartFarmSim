## Recommendation

Implement a **new, short canopy–air–vapor dynamic subsystem** first: independent canopy temperature, air temperature, and **vapor mass per floor area**; fixed positive LAI; explicit separate canopy and air heat inputs; and explicit, positive canopy vapor resistance. Reuse the accepted instantaneous exchange kernel at each common solver stage. Vapor mass is equivalent to humidity ratio when dry-air mass per floor area is fixed, and avoids an unnecessary dry-air-mass parameter in this first child. This is a small dynamic exchange child of `crop-climate-coupling`. It does **not** complete dynamic crop growth feedback or the parent task.

Do not feed its evaporation output into the old aggregate Euler engine and call the result two-way physical coupling. That engine already includes the canopy in its sensible heat store and debits latent heat. A separate canopy store attached to it would duplicate stores or heat transfers. Reading stored growth LAI/forcing and replaying them through a climate calculation is prescribed-input replay; it cannot feed a new climate trajectory back into the already immutable growth result. These conclusions follow from the current [canopy contract](../contracts/crop-canopy-exchange-v1.md), [thermal contract](../contracts/thermal-v1.md), and [crop cycle execution contract](../contracts/crop-cycle-execution-v1.md).

After the small subsystem is accepted, implement a separate joint crop RHS/short integrator that derives LAI from the *current trial crop state*, sends the dynamic canopy temperature to crop rates at that same stage, and integrates both crop temperature clocks. Before that step, settle the energy meaning of changing canopy heat capacity and leaf removal. Only then extend continuation/storage/replay under new identities. The existing full synthetic replay remains a useful software predecessor; it supplies neither these missing physical inputs nor field validation.

## Proposed first dynamic child's equations

The following is a **project reduction derived for a synthetic software experiment**, not a transcription of the complete GreenLight climate model. Keep it a new version. A single short constant-forcing interval is sufficient for its first acceptance; choose exact resource and solver budgets in the implementation contract after examining its rates.

Prefer a per-floor-area formulation. State is `(t_c, t_a, m_v)`: independent canopy/air temperatures in °C and vapor mass `m_v >= 0` in kg_water/m²_floor. Gas-law temperature is explicitly `T_a = t_a + 273.15` K. Let `h_air = V/A > 0` [m³/m²_floor] be the fixed modeled air-volume/floor-area ratio; use one explicit positive constant `rhoAirCap` for both capacity and the accepted exchange call. Let `c_leaf > 0` [J/(m²_leaf·K)] be an explicit input, with constant `cap_can = c_leaf*LAI` and `cap_air = h_air*rhoAirCap*cPAir` [J/(m²_floor·K)]. LAI and canopy vapor resistance are explicit positive constants. This reuses source capacity forms while keeping their extra parameters explicit; the proposed numerical values remain **null / input hold** until the new synthetic input/profile is reviewed.

Let `q_c` be net externally supplied canopy heat [W/m²_floor], excluding H/LE; let `q_a` be net externally supplied air sensible heat [W/m²_floor], excluding H and canopy latent heat; and let `f_v` be signed external vapor mass input [kg_water/m²_floor/s]. Explicitly identify the heat/radiation/envelope/ground/ventilation/heater paths and water boundary contributing to these inputs, including every declared zero. A declared vapor forcing alone is not a ventilation or HVAC model. E/H/LE below retain the accepted kernel's per-floor-area units; `LE = lambda*E` uses the **same** lambda as the vapor ledger.

```text
e_air = m_v * R_v * (t_a + 273.15) / h_air

cap_can * dt_c/dt = q_c - H - LE
cap_air * dt_a/dt = q_a + H
          dm_v/dt = E + f_v
```

For a prescribed indirect heater, supplied heat belongs in `q_a`; no direct combustion vapor/CO₂ source is added. For the first child, explicitly supplied heat is simpler than reusing the old end-target controller. A later controller must be separately specified for this coupled system. Existing aggregate solar absorption does not define `q_c/q_a`; first use explicit owned synthetic net heat forcings and describe the omitted radiation/surface dynamics. Whole-zone reporting requires explicit A and multiplies each per-floor store/flux exactly once; it does not change these equations. No area parameter is needed merely to solve this synthetic per-floor subsystem when `h_air` is already explicit.

For a reference temperature tref, the model bookkeeping store per floor area is

```text
u* = cap_can*(t_c-tref) + cap_air*(t_a-tref) + lambda*m_v
du*/dt = q_c + q_a + lambda*f_v
```

This is obtained by summing the three equations: ±H cancel, and canopy latent debit cancels vapor latent credit. Record each state's integrated ledger: canopy change minus integral(q_c−H−LE), air sensible change minus integral(q_a+H), and vapor change minus integral(E+f_v). Record the combined `u*` change minus integral(q_c+q_a+lambda*f_v). All have explicit per-floor units (J/m²_floor or kg_water/m²_floor), with the same accepted solver weights. A small residual tests this declared bookkeeping. It does not account for omitted water/vapor sensible enthalpy, leaf-water storage and replacement, photosynthetic chemical energy, pressure work, or cover/floor stores. The water boundary is an explicit adequate-water canopy source/reservoir, not irrigation or purchased water. Record `−E` on that external canopy-water boundary and `+E` in vapor. Their sum cancellation does not prove the mass or adequacy of an unmodeled crop liquid-water store. Track E as modeled vapor transfer; do not report it as whole-farm water use.

The moisture coordinate interoperability is consequential. If dry-air mass per floor area `mu_da=M/A` is fixed, then `m_v=mu_da*w`; an explicitly modeled ventilation source would be `f_v=(F/A)*(w_out-w)`. Thus the per-floor formulation is equivalent to the proposed whole-zone humidity-ratio store. At fixed M/V, differentiating `e_air = (M/V)*R_v*T_a*w` gives

```text
de_air/dt = (R_v*T_a/V)*[F*(w_out-w)+A*E] + (e_air/T_a)*dT_a/dt
```

Omitting the final temperature term produces a different closure. Copying a source vapor-pressure capacity RHS into this humidity-ratio model is therefore not a mere unit conversion. The perfect-gas moist-air basis and distinct dry-air/water-vapor properties are described by [ASHRAE psychrometrics](https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/F17/SI/f17_ch01/f17_ch01_si.aspx); the derivative above is this project's algebraic inference from its fixed-mass/fixed-volume closure.

For this first child, use one registered canopy saturation law for the exchange and both air/leaf checks. Both canopy and air must remain within **10–34°C inclusive**, the accepted kernel's synthetic window; gas-law T_a must remain positive and m_v nonnegative. Apply every guard at the initial state, every numerical trial/stage, and each accepted endpoint. For an evaporation-only scope, recommend hold on `e_air >= p_sat(t_a)` or `e_air >= p_sat(t_c)`, including equality, rather than inferring a liquid film. Preserve the failing time/stage and last confirmed state. Do not clip RH, E, m_v, or temperatures; do not convert a held trial into a checkpoint. If the implementation instead permits signed E, it must explicitly adopt a **mathematical reverse-exchange research scope** and refrain from claiming a leaf condensation/liquid-water model; bulk undersaturation alone establishes no cold-leaf/cover condensation absence. The [NWS PDF](https://www.weather.gov/media/epz/wxcalc/vaporPressure.pdf) verifies the old thermal law's Celsius/hPa/base-10 form; the [Conservation Physics page](https://www.conservationphysics.org/atmcalc/atmoclc1.html) verifies the alternative 238.3 denominator used by the canopy profile. This review provides no new empirical accuracy range for either law.

## Why full crop feedback follows as another slice

With a changing LAI and `C_c = A*c_leaf*LAI`, a store `U_c = C_c*(T_c-Tref)` obeys

```text
dU_c/dt = C_c*dT_c/dt + (T_c-Tref)*dC_c/dt
```

The second term does not disappear because the leaf-carbon ledger balances. Growth, water supply, and leaf removal change the material/energy boundary; an event may remove canopy sensible energy along with area. The new joint model must either define those transport terms and source temperatures, or adopt a separately declared approximation with a precise limited conservation claim. This audit does not invent that physiology or reinterpret carbon mass as leaf heat capacity. A constant effective canopy capacity while LAI evolves is also a new approximation requiring its own explicit review; it cannot be attributed to the source's LAI-dependent capacity.

The source does define an LAI-dependent canopy capacity and a leaf-area heat-capacity parameter, but neither is part of the accepted nine-parameter instantaneous profile. The same source's stomatal resistance expression adds radiation/CO₂/vapor-pressure dependencies beyond the accepted kernel, and its declared switch-slope unit warrants the existing unresolved dimension review. These facts are checked against [the pinned source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/davkat1/GreenLight/7a7b36870135aa38bbe81e65590dcf5473786bdd/greenlight/models/katzin_2021/definition/vanthoor_2011/greenhouse_vanthoor_2011_chapter_8.json); no new coefficient or closure is adopted here.

A later joint RHS must evaluate from one common trial state/time: current leaf→LAI→E/H/LE; current Tcan→growth/respiration/T24/Tsum; then all derivatives and ledgers. Passing yesterday's stored LAI into today's climate RHS or holding Tcan fixed across crop RK4 stages introduces a lag/splitting approximation. Any such approximation needs a new scheme and convergence evidence. The existing crop forcing packet cannot express this feedback merely by placing `reference_calculation` on a constant segment.
