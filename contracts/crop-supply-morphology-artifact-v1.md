# Repeated supply-limited morphology artifact, research v1

Bounded synthetic storage/reader acceptance is recorded in
[the verification report](../research/crop-supply-morphology-artifact-20261030.md).
This adds immutable storage and a calculation-free reader for the accepted repeated
family; it does not approve a cultivar or cycle.

`produce_supply_morphology_artifact(directory, access, *, segments, terminal)`
takes ordered genuine repeated Contexts and genuine checkpoints for every confirmed
original-grid endpoint. Each segment starts at its actual post-material seed.
Cold-restored checkpoints must use trusted bytes/hashes. Never manufacture older
typed handles. Preserve source Context/CP bytes and every geometry/carbon/thermal
origin. Adjacent Contexts must be the actual single next adoption from the previous
last CP, with identical root/grid, one schedule consumption and exact receipt link.
No prefix before the supplied initial checkpoint is invented.

The producer copies original CAS inputs, pinned profiles/request and license notice,
and stores Context/CP and compact material evidence without rewriting them. It uses
the existing POSIX blob store and its access, symlink, single-link and size checks.
Publish a root last after complete read-only graph validation and current genuine
Context/code fences. Same inputs/code produce the same root; failed publication
may leave unreferenced blobs, never a completed root. Explicit numerical hold may
publish only the last confirmed prefix with the original hold, not a completed run.

`read_supply_morphology_manifest` and `read_supply_morphology_frame` require a root
hash from the trusted producer/run record. Read current files/code/embedded grants
before and after projection; reject missing, changed, replaced or inconsistent data.
They import no growth/RK4/point/initialization modules and perform no RHS, CLI, DB,
network or writes. Recorded arithmetic QC and clock/quantity composition are allowed;
neither geometry potential nor actual dimensions may be recomputed from a curve.

Frames include the exact confirmed CP states, CP-less dense-before material states
and actual after-material seeds, in original order with exact rational elapsed time,
UTC origin, phase, event sequence, cursor/prefix and source hashes. At the same
material instant, before precedes after. Expose stored per-organ A/L/C, represented
age and exact birth-relative clock, geometry origins/deficits and global accounts.
Same source values have one authority. Preserve optional leaves and explicit parents.
Do not infer width, pose, fruit growth, harvest or missing intermediate states.

Root/Context/frame/response are each at most2MiB; CP512KiB, source16MiB,
store64MiB/4096files, inventory/frames512 and sample scalars32768. Keep existing lower
caps. A bounded stored interval is not full-cycle or maximum-inventory acceptance.
False redistribution stays false; export/SDK/realistic3D follow this boundary.

Source grants are self-contained research copies plus current local file checks.
External farm custody/revocation, runtime admission, signature/publication authority,
G0–G4 and R2 remain separate. A self-rehashed replacement root does not prove the
original execution or scientific correctness. Synthetic results retain explicit
research holds and cannot claim crop forecasts, purchases, margins or ranking.

Acceptance: actual repeated results through the original horizon, independent cold
reader and exact values/IDs/clocks/phases, root-last/retry/partial failure,
tampered/rehashed state/unit/order/parent/receipt/context/grant/code and late file
checks, zero growth imports/RHS/CLI/DB/network/write on read, and original source,
assets/services and WSL preservation. Whole product integration remains subsequent.
