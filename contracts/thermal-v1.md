# Thermal v1: synthetic G1 calculation and trace contract

Status: proposed `PROJECT_AGGREGATE` model, scoped to a self-authored synthetic G1 software-contract trace. `WUR_ORIGINAL` names the distinct research reference, not this model. Version `thermal-v1` is not a validated greenhouse model. The source distinctions and unresolved inputs are in [thermal-sources.md](thermal-sources.md). A deterministic engine and server, not generated prose or this schema alone, must calculate and check every value. A synthetic fixture may carry a chosen real coordinate in its linked input snapshot or manifest; that coordinate does not establish local weather, facility calibration, or field accuracy. Actual external-source runs and field-accuracy claims need applicable real-source G0 checks and later gates, including independent measurements and a cover/screen surface model where relevant. This synthetic-only accepted-trace schema cannot accept actual external weather or facility inputs.

## Boundary, states, and inputs

There is **one thermal control volume**, a well-mixed aggregate of greenhouse dry air **and canopy**. Its only dynamic states are aggregate temperature `T` [K] and humidity ratio `w` [kg_v/kg_da]. `C_eff` [J/K] is the effective sensible heat capacity of precisely this aggregate. The canopy's latent debit is taken from this same capacity (`latent_draw_from_aggregate`), while canopy-to-air sensible transfer is internal and must not appear again. `Q_solar` is absorbed power entering this aggregate; radiation deposited in cover, soil, or an omitted store cannot be included in it. Cover and soil are external flux boundaries, never hidden dynamic thermal states. The fixed dry-air mass `M_da` [kg_da] and indoor volume `V` [m³] are independent, explicit inputs. Moisture storage is `M_da*w` [kg_v]. Vapor and liquid sensible enthalpy, variable heat capacity, spatial gradients, and CO₂ are omitted model approximations.

Every facility, physical, forcing, first initial-state, control, and numerical **value**, including a chosen zero, carries `value`, exact `unit`, `origin`, `basis_ref`, and `version`. `basis_ref` resolves to an immutable source/assumption record; `origin=assumed` is visible and cannot be presented as measured. Generic sourced numeric records and saturation-law coefficients allow only `measured`, `literature`, or `assumed`; heater availability allows only `measured` or `assumed`. This v1 accepted-trace schema permits `origin=derived` for forcing `outdoor_humidity_ratio` [kg_v/kg_da] and `solar_gain` [W], and for the narrowly defined carried initial temperature and humidity ratio in a later trace slice. Each derived forcing value also names a nonempty `calculation_rule_ref`, its `calculation_rule_version`, and nonempty `input_record_ids` of immutable inputs; an assumed output cannot bypass either calculation. A derived generic input or saturation-law coefficient requires a later contract version with explicit derivation provenance and a deterministic rule. Engine-calculated states and trace outputs are separate from sourced inputs. The server resolves the accepted derived references and checks record immutability, rule versions, chronology, and arithmetic. No coefficients, saturation-pressure formula, tolerance, weather gap fill, or control default may be supplied silently. Inputs and their raw/normalized versions, source times, QC, rights, reviewer, hashes, manifest, decision ID, code/environment version, and explicit holds remain immutable. A correction creates a new version. For an accepted synthetic trace, `synthetic_input_review_id` identifies a scoped review of the self-authored fixture's author, synthetic label, source locator and fixture ID, normalization rule, units, hash, rights, QC assertions, and reviewer. The server verifies those records and their links before G1 calculation/replay; this review is **not** an external-source G0 pass. Actual-source G0 remains on hold until its separate evidence passes.

In this **synthetic-only** trace shape, every `source_records[*]` entry must contain `observed_start_utc: null` and `observed_end_utc: null`. These are explicit declarations that no observation occurred; omitting them or replacing them with the hypothetical dates is invalid. Separate required `applicability_start_utc` and `applicability_end_utc` fields describe the half-open UTC interval `[start,end)` for which that self-authored input applies. For the existing weather and parameter fixtures, the [manifest v2](../fixtures/manifest-v2.json) pins a hypothetical `2026-10-15T08:00:00Z` to `2026-10-15T10:00:00Z` applicability interval; the [weather fixture](../fixtures/synthetic-weather-v1.json) splits it into two contiguous forcing intervals. These dates are not weather observations. Keep `thermal-v1`: no accepted trace has been published under this version.

`source_records[*].original_unit` is a per-field object, never a string rendering of a dictionary. The weather source map is exactly `{T_o: K, phi_o: 1, p_o: Pa, solar_interval_energy: J/m²}`; the thermal parameter source map is exactly `{temperature: K, humidity_ratio: kg_v/kg_da, heat: W_th, time: s}`. JSON Schema rejects missing, extra, or invalid fields and units. The deterministic server also compares each source's entire map to the corresponding `variable_units` object in the raw-byte-pinned manifest v2 entry, so exchanging two otherwise valid maps is a hold.

For fixture-linked derived forcing, `outdoor_humidity_ratio` uses registered `calculation_rule_ref=thermal-outdoor-w-v1` and `solar_gain` uses `calculation_rule_ref=thermal-solar-interval-v1`. Both use `calculation_rule_version=v1` and output `version=v1`. Each `basis_ref` identifies its registered rule object in the immutable thermal fixture: `synthetic-thermal-parameters-v1:/conversion_rules/outdoor_humidity_ratio` or `synthetic-thermal-parameters-v1:/conversion_rules/solar_gain`. The server resolves that fixture through the manifest SHA-256 pin and compares the trace's ref, versions, basis, and exact per-hour input IDs with the registered rule and interval plan. A missing, arbitrary, or exchanged rule or basis is a hold; these fixture links do not imply an engine result.

The schema has one interval and one constant forcing per trace. The pinned two-hour fixture therefore needs two adjacent one-hour traces with distinct `trace_id`s, consecutive `trace_sequence_index` values 0 and 1, and the same `run_id`, `model_version`, `parameter_set_version`, `manifest_sha256`, `engine_version`, `unit_registry_version`, input snapshot, and decision. The server must verify that the ordered trace set covers the pinned fixture start to end without a gap or duplicate hour before accepting the Run; a lone first-hour trace is incomplete. Each weather source record identifies its specific hour and applies only to that hour; the thermal parameter record may span both. Every step stays inside its own trace, forcing hour, and applicable source intervals. The first trace (index 0) uses the sourced fixture initial state. Every later trace's temperature and humidity ratio use `origin=derived`, carry rule `thermal-state-carry-v1`/`v1`, and `version=v1`. Their canonical immutable `basis_ref` is `trace-sha256:<64 lowercase hex>#/steps/<last-index>/state_end/<field>`, where the hash is SHA-256 of the **complete immutable raw previous trace bytes** and `<field>` is exactly `temperature` or `humidity_ratio`. The same trace also carries the previous `trace_id`, that SHA-256 in `previous_trace_sha256`, and that field pointer in `previous_state_pointer`. The server retrieves those exact bytes and requires `basis_ref` to equal `trace-sha256:` plus the computed hash plus `#` plus the actual last-step field pointer; a self-declared basis cannot replace that comparison. It also checks the previous trace's hash and identity, adjacent endpoints, consecutive indices, matching run/model/parameter/manifest/engine/unit identity, and equality of each prior final value and unit with the carried initial value and unit. This basis identifies a derived state in the previous trace, not a measured source or an accepted Run. A sourced value labeled `assumed` cannot stand in for this carry. This linkage describes a candidate shape; it does not establish that an engine calculated either trace.

JSON Schema checks the required nulls, field types, carry-reference shape, and UTC text shape. Its `date-time` format may be annotation-only under [Draft 2020-12 Validation](https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/json-schema-validation), and it cannot compare arbitrary timestamp fields. Before accepting any candidate, the deterministic server must parse calendar-valid UTC instants and require `applicability_start_utc < applicability_end_utc`, `interval.start_utc < interval.end_utc`, and every step and relevant forcing interval to have positive duration and fall within its own trace and relevant source interval. It must resolve each source record ID and each derived `input_record_ids` through the fixture's `input_records` to the JSON pointer in raw bytes pinned by manifest v2. The humidity result must use exactly that hour's `T_o`, `phi_o`, `p_o`, both gas constants, and saturation law; the solar result must use exactly that hour's solar energy and interval endpoints, floor area, and absorbed fraction. Missing, extra, swapped, or cross-hour IDs are a hold. The server verifies manifest and raw-byte SHA-256 pins, fixture interval provenance, and both adjacent forcing intervals, and rejects invented observations, gaps, overlaps, substitutions, or mismatched source claims. It checks publication, availability, and retrieval instants against the immutable decision record's time, including any archived decision-time evidence needed for a later retrieval. It independently verifies QC, author, reviewer, normalization, rights, and review scope. Missing or contradictory proof is a hold, never an accepted Run; schema validity alone proves none of chronology, rights, or pins.

`F` is the **dry-air** ventilation mass flow [kg_da/s]. Equal inward and outward dry-air flows are a synthetic closure assumption, so `M_da` remains constant. `F` is specified exogenously for the interval. An opening fraction, wind speed, or fan command alone is not an accepted conversion to true airflow. `E_canopy` [kg_v/s] is nonnegative and specified exogenously; it is not a crop growth or water-use forecast. The signed ground flux `Q_ground` [W] is positive **into** the aggregate. Envelope conductance `K_env` [W/K], dry-air heat capacity `c_da` [J/(kg_da*K)], and latent heat `lambda` [J/kg_v] must have versioned bases. The use of constant `lambda` and `c_da` over the declared temperature range is an explicit approximation.

For outdoor weather, normalize `T_o` [K], relative humidity `phi_o` [1], and outdoor total pressure `p_o` [Pa] from the same sourced interval and a versioned saturation-pressure law `p_sat(T)` [Pa]. With `epsilon=R_da/R_v`, calculate `e_o=phi_o*p_sat(T_o)` and `w_o=epsilon*e_o/(p_o-e_o)`. The `outdoor_humidity_ratio.input_record_ids` must resolve to the required weather, gas-constant, and saturation-law inputs. Reject `p_o <= e_o`, invalid humidity, law-domain errors, missing QC/rights, or mismatched applicable forcing intervals. Retain `T_o`, `phi_o`, `p_o`, `w_o`, and conversion-rule provenance. Indoor **total** pressure is derived, not fixed to outdoor pressure: `p_i=(M_da/V)*(R_da+w*R_v)*T`; indoor vapor pressure is `e_i=(M_da/V)*w*R_v*T`, and `RH_i=e_i/p_sat(T)`. `R_da`, `R_v`, the saturation law and every numerical coefficient of that law must be registered and versioned before a fixture can run. Do not force `p_i=p_o` while also fixing `M_da` and `V`. Recompute indoor pressure and RH from each state; heating at fixed `w` does not preserve RH. These are project closure choices informed by [ASHRAE psychrometrics](https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/F17/SI/f17_ch01/f17_ch01_si.aspx), not a copied WUR equation.

If solar input is interval energy `H_solar` [J/m²] over exactly `[start_utc,end_utc)`, normalize once as `Q_solar=H_solar*A_floor*f_abs/(end_utc-start_utc)` [W], where floor area `A_floor` [m²] and absorbed fraction `f_abs` [1], bounded from zero to one, are explicit sourced values. The `solar_gain.input_record_ids` must resolve to that energy, area, fraction, and interval record. A source in MJ/m² is first converted to J/m² with a versioned unit conversion. Never treat MJ/m² as W/m² or repeat an interval total in every substep. The normalized power is held constant only across the matching interval; no endpoint interpolation is inferred. Missing or negative solar energy is a hold unless a separately evidenced signed sensor correction rule applies. A declared zero solar/ground/canopy/ventilation input is still a sourced assumption.

## Frozen substep equations and heater control

For each positive UTC substep `dt` [s], freeze forcing and control availability, then evaluate all unheated terms at the **start** state `(T_n,w_n)`. Each interval is partitioned into positive contiguous substeps; `end_utc > start_utc`, the first step starts at interval start, the last ends at interval end, and adjacent endpoints are equal. No unannounced interpolation or midstep weather substitution is allowed. The signed unheated sensible power [W] is

`Q_0 = Q_solar + K_env*(T_o-T_n) + Q_ground + F*c_da*(T_o-T_n) - lambda*E_canopy`.

The heater target is the **end of this same substep**. With setpoint `T_set` [K], calculate unlimited nonnegative demand **before heater input** as `Q_demand=max(0, C_eff*(T_set-T_n)/dt - Q_0)` [W_th]. `P_cap` [W_th] means maximum **heat delivered to the aggregate**, not electrical or fuel input. `heater.available` is a sourced dimensionless control value with `value` of type boolean, `unit="1"`, `origin` (`measured` or `assumed`), immutable `basis_ref`, and `version`; there is no separate availability basis field. For `mode=indirect_sensible`, `Q_delivered=0` when `heater.available.value=false` and otherwise `min(max(Q_demand,0),P_cap)`; `Q_unmet=max(Q_demand-Q_delivered,0)`. Thus `0 <= Q_delivered <= P_cap`. A negative raw target requirement is zero demand, never active cooling. Availability and target are frozen at the substep start; recompute demand from each new state on the next substep. The moisture source `heater_vapor=0` [kg_v] is an explicit supported-mode rule. Direct combustion air heaters and any heater with water injection or an unknown moisture path trigger `DIRECT_HEATER_HOLD`; the WUR original includes direct-heater vapor input. Neither heater capacity nor delivered heat establishes purchased `kWh_e`, fuel, cost, or a COP/efficiency. Those require their own metering and conversion evidence.

Before any condensation, the proposed `PROJECT_AGGREGATE` explicit-Euler equations are:

```text
M_da*(w_{n+1}-w_n) = dt*[ F*(w_o-w_n) + E_canopy ]                [kg_v]
C_eff*(T_{n+1}-T_n) = dt*[ Q_0 + Q_delivered ]                    [J]
```

Ventilation sensible exchange is `F*c_da*(T_o-T_n)` [W]. The canopy latent term `-lambda*E_canopy` is debited **once** from the air-plus-canopy aggregate. This is a project reduction, not Vanthoor Eq 2.1/2.2 or a validated Korean greenhouse coefficient set. A direct heater vapor source or surface condensation cannot be added without a new model version.

## Condensation and acceptance

Policy `hold_on_saturation` permits only synthetic software-contract traces, including one whose linked fixture names a chosen coordinate. At the initial state and each candidate endpoint, derive `e_i` and `p_sat(T)` using the versioned law. If `e_i >= p_sat(T)` (bulk saturation reached), or the state is nonfinite, `T <= 0`, `w < 0`, or the law is out of domain, stop with `CONDENSATION_HOLD`; do not clamp RH to 100%, delete vapor, or invent latent return. The ledger's `condensation` is exactly zero for every accepted step. **Bulk undersaturation does not establish absence of condensation on a colder cover or screen.** This model has no cover/screen temperature or heat path and cannot calculate surface condensation, its latent return, or a field-safe humidity path. Actual external-source runs and field-accuracy claims remain on hold pending their real-source G0, a separate surface model where relevant, independent measurements, and later gates, even when all synthetic bulk steps stay undersaturated.

The engine must reject nonpositive durations, gaps/overlaps, invalid or unapproved synthetic metadata, missing values, inconsistent units, impossible parameter signs, absent numerical rules, and missing or failed synthetic input review. `source_records[*].synthetic=true`, project QC `pass`, self-owned use/display/redistribution rights `allowed`, and provider QC other than `fail` are structural conditions for this accepted trace. They do not prove the author, hash, rights, QC, normalization, or record linkage is true; the server checks those facts. An external record marked `synthetic=false` or disguised as synthetic cannot be accepted here. The separate actual-source path must check its applicable G0 evidence before use. `dt*F/M_da <= 1` and `dt*(K_env+F*c_da)/C_eff <= 1` are necessary frozen-coefficient nonoscillation screens for this explicit scheme, not general stability or accuracy proofs. Reject a step that violates either, or that fails finite-state, saturation, dimensional, or registered step-convergence checks. A step-convergence check recomputes the same interval as two half steps with frozen external forcing but controller demand recomputed at each half-step start, then compares end `T` [K], `w` [kg_v/kg_da], and delivered energy [J]. Its **fixture-specified, unit-bearing** limits, rationale, rule version, and comparison outcome must precede execution. The fixture similarly supplies absolute vapor [kg_v] and aggregate-energy [J] residual limits with rationale and version before the run; no numerical tolerance is invented here. An acceptance check cannot be passed merely by choosing a very loose limit after seeing output. Fail closed if no defensible fixture rule exists. The stiff system described in WUR's original motivates this caution; these two screens do not prove coupled numerical convergence.

For every accepted substep, record signed integrated mass terms [kg_v]: `ventilation=dt*F*(w_o-w_n)`, `canopy_evaporation=dt*E_canopy`, `heater_vapor=0`, `condensation=0`. Record signed sensible heat terms [J]: `solar=dt*Q_solar`, `heater=dt*Q_delivered`, `envelope=dt*K_env*(T_o-T_n)`, `ground=dt*Q_ground`, `ventilation_sensible=dt*F*c_da*(T_o-T_n)`, `canopy_latent=-dt*lambda*E_canopy`. Positive means into the corresponding aggregate store. The two ledger residuals are

```text
vapor_residual = M_da*(w_{n+1}-w_n) - sum(mass_terms)              [kg_v]
aggregate_energy_residual = C_eff*(T_{n+1}-T_n)
  + lambda*M_da*(w_{n+1}-w_n)
  - [sum(heat_terms) + lambda*sum(mass_terms)]                     [J]
```

The second expression is the algebraic residual of **this** aggregate sensible-plus-vapor bookkeeping: `C_eff*(T-T_ref)+lambda*M_da*w`. The `-lambda*E_canopy` sensible debit and `+lambda*E_canopy` stored-vapor credit cancel. It is not a full physical energy balance for omitted water/vapor sensible enthalpy, radiation paths, or cover stores; a near-zero residual is not field accuracy. The server recomputes equations, ledgers, bounds, timestamps, references, source rights, and gate status rather than trusting trace values or JSON Schema alone. A held candidate is recorded with reason and cannot be published as an accepted Run.

Delivered heat energy per step is `Q_delivered*dt/3,600,000` [kWh_th]; sum only accepted steps for interval delivered heat. `1 kWh = 3.6 MJ` is the [NIST conversion](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors/nist-guide-si-appendix-b8). `kWh_th` is modeled supplied heat; `kWh_e` is purchased electricity and is outside this trace. G1 checks calculation/replay only. G2 needs independent local temperature/humidity and relevant heat measurements; later crop, economic, comparison, and deployment claims need their own gates.
