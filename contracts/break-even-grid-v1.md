# Conditional break-even grid candidate v1

Status: synthetic software candidate. The [full break-even requirement](../docs/ECONOMICS.md) remains open. This contract evaluates only an explicit, finite set of pre-pinned user-assumption scenarios. It does not prove a continuous-range root, a feasible sales target, a future margin, or a crop recommendation.

The server-owned `BreakEvenPlan` binds one tenant and request digest to 2–256 exact Decimal grid values, distinct immutable market scenario references, one fixed economic-input projection, and one fixed joint-shock projection. The request fixes the baseline, unavailable market context, D, evaluation calendar, target (`oi`, `operating_cash`, or `cumulative_equity_cash`), variable (`kg` or `KRW/kg`), sale ID/batch/grade/channel, sale and collection event times, and exact minimum/maximum/step. The selected sale variable and linked collection amount may differ by trial; all other fields in the fixed projections, including the contract capacity, must agree. The client does not submit computed results or choose the repository.

For every grid value, `BreakEvenService.scan` calls `MarketScenarioService.calculate_pinned`. That path reloads and checks the immutable baseline, joint shock, rights, settlement applicability, derived economic scenario and full date-by-date ledger. The scanner then checks the resulting sale amount, scenario and plan hashes, fixed projections, decision clock, calendar and context. It reports the three economic targets separately, along with the underlying result IDs and cash shortage fields. If any target is unresolved, the scan returns `hold` without partial trial output.

`zero_on_grid` means an exact zero was calculated at a listed value. `no_zero_on_grid` means no listed value equals zero; it says nothing about values between points. `bracket_only` records adjacent opposite signs without fabricating an interpolated root. `nonmonotone_on_grid` records observed direction reversal and supplies all observed zeros/brackets, without claiming all roots. Every result remains a conditional user-assumption calculation with Assessment `hold`; none of these statuses predicts attainable sales, cash or profit.

The [focused tests](../backend/tests/test_break_even.py) use self-authored synthetic baseline and demand/supply/macro shocks, and verify full scenario and ledger recomputation for price and kg, the three target fields, exact zero, grid-only absence, sign bracket, nonmonotone cash, contract-capacity drift, foreign and repeated trial pins, and grid bounds. Production acceptance still needs a durable trusted plan/trial generator and result record, an independent `gpt-6-sol`/`xhigh` economic and architecture review, and a method that proves all roots or a justified no-root result over each permitted continuous interval. The task stays unchecked until that evidence exists.
