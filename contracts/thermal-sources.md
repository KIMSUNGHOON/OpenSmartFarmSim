## Synthetic inputs and upstream numerical bounds

The fixture explicitly assumes `C_eff=1,000,000 J/K`, `M_da=1,000 kg_da`, `V=850 m³`, `K_env=200 W/K`, `A_floor=100 m²`, `f_abs=0.5`, `F=0.02 kg_da/s`, `E_canopy=1e-5 kg_v/s`, `Q_ground=0 W`, initial `T=293 K`, `w=0.008 kg_v/kg_da`, and an available indirect sensible heater with `500 W_th` cap and `293 K` setpoint. Rounded `R_da=287 J/(kg_da*K)`, `R_v=461 J/(kg_v*K)`, `c_da=1000 J/(kg_da*K)`, and `lambda=2,500,000 J/kg_v` are assumptions, not site measurements or adopted literature properties. Every scalar including zero has an origin, basis, unit, and version. The first 3,600 s hypothetical weather interval is 290 K, 50% RH, 100,000 Pa, and 3,600 J/m² solar; the second is 289 K, 60% RH, 100,000 Pa, and zero solar. Their raw bytes remain pinned in manifest v1 and v2.

The versioned `thermal-outdoor-w-v1` conversion first applies the Kelvin-to-Celsius offset and hPa-to-Pa factor, then `e_o=phi_o*p_sat_Pa` and `w_o=(R_da/R_v)*e_o/(p_o-e_o)`. Its immutable input IDs include the saturation rule and all weather fields. `thermal-solar-interval-v1` divides interval solar energy by the linked duration and multiplies by area and absorbed fraction. Neither conversion copies or alters the weather file. At 289, 290, and 293 K the registered NWS expression yields about 1801.45933, 1919.93679, and 2317.30640 Pa, respectively; it is monotone across the selected range. The outdoor humidity ratios are 0.006034303321 for the first 290 K interval and 0.006802623257 kg_v/kg_da for the second 289 K interval.

With the fixed heater cap active throughout the upstream envelope, the equilibrium temperatures are `292.386363636 K` and `291.159090909 K`; humidity equilibria are `0.006534303321` and `0.007302623257 kg_v/kg_da`. Positive Euler weights at 60 and 30 s keep `291.159090909<=T<=293 K` and `0.006534303321<=w<=0.008 kg_v/kg_da`. The indoor maximum vapor pressure divided by the minimum NWS saturation pressure in that envelope is `0.6154042773 < 1`. This is only a bulk synthetic undersaturation bound; no cover/screen surface temperature is modeled.

For the 60-versus-two-30 s check, `thermal-euler-stability-v1` uses `dt*(K_env+F*c_da)/C_eff=0.0132` and `dt*F/M_da=0.0012`. The fixed-coefficient half-step differences are bounded by `(dt*a/2)^2*max|initial−equilibrium|`: `8.019e-5 K` and `5.276509e-10 kg_v/kg_da`. The preregistered limits remain `8.1e-5 K` and `5.4e-10 kg_v/kg_da`, including binary64 margin. With an active cap, delivered heat is exactly `500*60=2*500*30 J`, so its limit remains `0 J`.

For the residuals, `thermal-binary64-residual-v1` includes both endpoint storage magnitudes; an upstream bound gives mass operand sum `<16.1 kg_v` and energy operand sum `<6.28e8 J`. Under a reviewed binary64 operation order with at most 64 relevant rounded operations, `u=2^-53` and `gamma_64=64u/(1−64u)` give `<1.144e-13 kg_v` and `<4.463e-6 J`. The preregistered absolute limits remain `1.2e-13 kg_v` and `4.5e-6 J`. Operation-order review is pending; if its assumptions fail, the residual criterion stays on hold and needs a newly registered version before use. These bounds precede any engine or Run and do not establish accuracy.
